Life & Exploration in Chicago

Chicago is home to world-renowned architecture, parks, and cultural landmarks. Watch the video below to explore some of the city's top attractions:

Inspired by these iconic spots, I have visited several featured locations during my time in the city:

  • Millennium Park: Enjoying iconic public art pieces like Cloud Gate and open-air green space.
  • Chicago Riverwalk: Walking along the Chicago River while enjoying outdoor dining and stunning views of the city's architecture.
  • Lincoln Park Zoo: Exploring one of the nation's oldest free zoos surrounded by nature.

Campus Life at Illinois Tech

Campus life at the Illinois Institute of Technology (IIT) provides a welcoming and collaborative environment.

  • Attending SOAR Orientation to adapt to the new academic system and campus culture.
  • Participating in the Korean Student Association (KSA) events to build community and share cultures.
  • Cooking and sharing traditional Korean food with friends in the campus residence halls.

Personal Reflections as Exchange Student

Studying at Illinois Tech in Chicago is a great experience for me. Living in a new country, using English every day, and meeting friends from different backgrounds have helped me gain confidence and prepare for my future.
